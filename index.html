Featured project

Cosignal

AI agents are starting to issue refunds, pay vendors and move funds, but finance teams can't trust what they can't review.

Cosignal sits between an agent and the money. Risky actions wait for a human to approve them, and every step goes into an audit trail showing what the agent did, what it saw and who signed off. You just tried it in the card at the top of this page. I presented Cosignal at the University of Cincinnati's Friday Seminar series.

  1. ProposeThe agent sends a payment or refund to Cosignal before it runs.
  2. CheckPolicy rules decide what runs automatically and what waits.
  3. Co-signA reviewer sees the full context and approves or rejects.
  4. RecordA tamper-evident log keeps every decision for auditors.
